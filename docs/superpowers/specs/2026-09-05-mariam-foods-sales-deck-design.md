## 1. Executive Overview & Strategic Purpose
The objective is to produce a commercial-grade, institutional B2B sales presentation deck for **Mariam Food Industries**, an Egyptian food processor and exporter based in Cairo. The deck is engineered specifically to convert international food commodity importers, foodservice/HoReCa procurement directors, restaurant/pizza chain commissary buyers, and supermarket private label category managers worldwide.

The presentation establishes corporate credibility, demonstrates substantial production capacity, highlights rigorous international certifications, details technical product specifications (calibers, drained weights, salinity, pH), and showcases the company's multi-format packaging flexibility (institutional A10 cans, industrial bulk barrels, and retail glass jars).

- **Headline Font:** Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans (Modern, confident geometric sans-serif).
- **Body & Metrics:** Clean tabular numerals for specifications (calibers, sizing, pallet configurations, drained weights).
- **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 16:9 widescreen canvas (`1920x1080` proportional layout), auto-scaling to match any display resolution smoothly.

### 2.3 Interactive Features & Print Engine
- **Slide Navigation:**
  - Forward/Back keyboard navigation (`ArrowRight`, `ArrowLeft`, `Space`, `Backspace`).
  - Next/Previous on-screen control dock with current slide progress tracker (`Slide X / 9`).
  - Slide thumbnail drawer / quick-jump menu for jumping directly between sections during live buyer pitches.
  - Fullscreen toggle button (`F` key or icon).
- **Product Inspection Lightbox:**
  - Clicking any product image launches a high-resolution magnification modal, enabling procurement teams to closely inspect slice thickness, pitting accuracy, and brine clarity.
- **Print / PDF Engine:**
  - Dedicated `@media print` rules: forced page-break per slide (`break-after: page`), removal of UI navigation chrome, crisp print background reproduction, high-contrast text rendering, and exact landscape dimensions (`A4` or `Letter` landscape) to allow immediate "Print to PDF" with one click.
